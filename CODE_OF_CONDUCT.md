# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

WhatCompanyStack is an open-source project built to help developers and tech enthusiasts learn more about the technologies used by companies, especially across Africa.

We want this to be a welcoming space where people can contribute, ask questions, share ideas, and learn from one another.

As contributors and maintainers, we are committed to making participation in this project a positive experience for everyone, regardless of their background, experience level, or location.

## Our Standards

We expect everyone participating in the WhatCompanyStack community to:

- Be respectful and considerate of others.
- Welcome people with different levels of experience.
- Give and receive constructive feedback.
- Ask questions and share ideas without fear of being judged.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.
- Be open to learning and helping others.
- Respect different opinions and approaches to solving problems.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not welcome in this community:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insults, or deliberately disrespectful comments.
- Sharing private information about others without their permission.
- Intimidation or any form of unwelcome behavior.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or contributions.
- Any other behavior that would reasonably make someone feel unwelcome or unsafe.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behavior that goes against this Code of Conduct, please let the maintainers know.

You can reach out to the maintainers through the project's communication channels or by contacting:

**Email:** chidideveloper@gmail.com

Please provide as much context as possible so we can properly understand and address the situation.

We will handle reports respectfully and make reasonable efforts to protect the privacy of everyone involved.

## Our Responsibilities

The maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the situation, maintainers may take appropriate action, including removing inappropriate comments, rejecting contributions, or restricting participation in the project.

Our goal is not to discourage participation but to ensure that WhatCompanyStack remains a healthy and welcoming open-source community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all WhatCompanyStack project spaces, including the GitHub repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community spaces related to the project.

## A Final Note

We are all here to build something useful, learn, and contribute to the ecosystem.

Be kind, be open to feedback, and remember that everyone starts somewhere.

Thank you for being part of WhatCompanyStack.
